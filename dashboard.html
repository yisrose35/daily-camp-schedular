What a plan does and does not copy

A plan copies your bunks, divisions, periods, bus routes, league setup and rotation history. Counsellors and parents never see it. When the session starts, make it official and it becomes the camp — what it replaces is kept so you can look back at it.

Campers, families and payments are never copied. Those are always the live ones, in every plan: there is no such thing as a draft payment.

Pricing

Registration checks today's date against the deadline automatically — a family sees and is charged the early-bird price only up through that date, then the regular tuition, with no staff action needed.

Sibling Discount

"Siblings" are all campers linked to the same family account, counted camp-wide across every session they're enrolled in. Whichever sibling has the single highest tuition in the family always pays full price; every other sibling gets the discount for the tier matching how many of the family's kids are enrolled in total — e.g. a row of "2 kids → 10%" and another of "3 kids → 12%" means a family with 3 enrolled kids discounts every sibling but the priciest one at 12%. The highest matching row applies (a 4th kid still uses the "3 kids" row if that's the highest one you've set). Stacks with Early Bird pricing.

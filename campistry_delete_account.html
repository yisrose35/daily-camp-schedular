Delete your account

Applies to both Campistry Link and Campistry Lite

You can request deletion of your account and the personal information associated with it at any time. This includes your login, profile, and app preferences. Once we confirm your request, we'll delete this information and let you know when it's done.

What isn't deleted automatically

  • Camper records tied to a camp's own roster — a camp may need to retain certain records (for example, health or enrollment history) as part of its own operations or legal obligations. If you want a specific camper's information removed, let us know and we'll route that to the camp along with your account deletion.
  • Messages sent to other people — a message you sent may remain visible to its recipient, the same way deleting your own copy of an email doesn't delete it from the other person's inbox.

To request deletion, email us the address below with the account email you'd like deleted. We reply to confirm we've received the request, and again once it's complete — typically within a few business days.

Email campistryoffice@gmail.com to request deletion

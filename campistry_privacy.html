Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy covers the Campistry platform, including Campistry Link (the parent portal) and Campistry Lite (the staff/counselor app), together "Campistry," "we," or "us." Campistry is provided to families and staff through a camp that has licensed it — the camp is the one who invites you, and who decides what Campistry is used for at that camp. This policy explains what we collect on the camp's behalf, why, and what choices you have.

Who Campistry is for. Campistry Link and Campistry Lite are used by parents/guardians and by adult camp staff — not by campers directly. Where this policy describes information about children (camper health notes, photos, emergency contacts), that information is provided and managed by an adult (a parent or camp staff member), not entered by a child using the app.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (used to sign in).
  • Display name, and — if provided to the camp — a phone number, kept on the camp's own family/staff roster.
  • If you enable biometric unlock (Face ID / fingerprint), the biometric scan itself never reaches Campistry or the camp — it is verified entirely on your device by your phone's operating system. We only ever receive a yes/no confirmation and a device-bound credential identifier.

Camper information (parents and staff)

Provided by parents or camp staff to help the camp care for a child: name, division/grade/bunk assignment, allergies, medications, dietary needs, doctor and insurance information, and emergency contacts.

Photos, and face-matching (opt-in)

  • Camps upload photos taken during the day, which parents can view of their own children.
  • To help parents find photos of their own child faster, Campistry offers an optional, opt-in face-matching feature. A parent who turns this on for their child provides a few reference photos. Matching is computed on the device, in the browser — the reference photos are never uploaded anywhere for this computation. What we store is a small numeric representation of the face (not a photo) and a small thumbnail, used only to find that child's photos faster. This feature is off unless a parent turns it on, and can be turned off again at any time.

Messages

Messages and announcements sent between camp staff and parents through the app, including any files or forms attached to them.

Canteen, shop, and payment-related activity

Canteen account balances and deposits, camp shop orders, and tips, where the camp has these features turned on. Campistry records the transaction details needed to run these features (amounts, items, timestamps); actual payment card handling, where applicable, is performed by the camp's own payment provider, not stored by Campistry.

Device and technical information

  • A push-notification token, so the app can send you alerts you've opted into — messages, payment reminders, canteen activity, new photos, and health updates. You can turn each of these off individually in Settings.
  • Basic device information (platform, app version) used to keep the app working correctly and to diagnose problems.

Preferences

In-app settings you choose, like notification toggles, haptics, and theme — stored so the app remembers your choices.

How we use this information

  • To operate the core features of Campistry: schedules, messaging, photos, canteen, camp shop, and health/emergency information, on behalf of the camp you're connected to.
  • To send the notifications you've opted into.
  • To let camp staff and a camper's own parent(s) — and no one else — see that camper's information.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including diagnosing and fixing problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use camper or family data for advertising.

Children's information

Campistry Link and Lite are not directed at, marketed to, or designed for use by children — accounts are created and used by adults (parents and camp staff). Information about campers is provided by those adults, on behalf of the camp, to support the camp's operation of its program. If you are a parent and believe your child has somehow created their own account, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

Data security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest through our infrastructure provider. Access to a camper's health, contact, and photo information is restricted to that camper's own parent(s)/guardian(s) and to camp staff with appropriate access — not to other families at the same camp.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account or your camp's use of Campistry is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. A camp may also have its own retention practices for records it is required to keep.

Your choices and rights

  • Notification preferences — turn individual notification types on or off any time in Settings.
  • Biometric unlock — turn on or off any time in Settings; turning it off removes the saved credential from your device.
  • Face-matching — opt in or out per child at any time.
  • Access, correction, or deletion — to request a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below, or contact your camp directly. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable time, subject to any records the camp or we are required to retain.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Campistry after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request regarding your information, can be sent to campistryoffice@gmail.com. You can also reach out to your camp directly, since they are the ones who invited you and administer your account.

Campistry — this policy applies to Campistry Link and Campistry Lite.
